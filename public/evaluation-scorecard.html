A 24-point pilot gate

Score what the system can prove.

Rate eight dimensions from 0 to 3. Use evidence from a real workflow—not a scripted demo. The score is a discussion tool, not a compliance determination or substitute for accountable human review.

0No evidence
1Claim or demo only
2Pilot evidence with gaps
3Repeatable, auditable evidence
Outcome definition 01

Outcome definition

The team agrees what “qualified, interested, and worth interviewing” means before reviewing results.

Brief fidelity 02

Brief fidelity

Must-haves, preferences, trade-offs, geography, and evidence of seniority are explicit and approved.

Search provenance 03

Search provenance

Sources, coverage, refresh limits, exclusions, and likely blind spots are documented for the role.

Match evidence 04

Match evidence

Every recommendation can be traced to role-relevant evidence, and reviewers can inspect rejects and borderline cases.

External action control 05

External action control

Sender identity, channel, message, timing, follow-up, suppression, and approval boundaries are visible and enforceable.

Candidate experience and accessibility 06

Candidate experience and accessibility

People can understand the process, request an accommodation, correct relevant data, opt out, and reach a human.

Handoff quality 07

Handoff quality

The hiring team receives evidence, current interest, unresolved questions, and a clear next step—not another raw queue.

Audit and fallback 08

Audit and fallback

Inputs, edits, approvals, actions, and outcomes remain distinguishable; automation can pause without losing the workflow.

0/24 Score every dimension to see a pilot recommendation.

Interpretation: 0–8 means stop; 9–16 supports only a narrow, reversible pilot; 17–24 means the system is ready to be validated on a real role. A high score is not proof of legal compliance, fairness, or business value.

Next step

Test the score against the actual workflow.

Ask the vendor to demonstrate every scored dimension using one role, including weak matches, corrections, approval points, and the final handoff.
